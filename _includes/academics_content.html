Academic Community

In my video production services tailored for the academic community, I bring a methodical approach to each project.

From inception, a refined subject research process underpins my pre-production planning. Using a demonstrated understanding of effective video communication, I collaborate closely with subject matter experts during the strategy phase to ensure their message is optimally conveyed through the video medium. This process generates a creative brief, technical brief, and script. Once we have these in hand, we proceed into the production phase.

During production, I handle setup and operation of cameras, sound, and lighting, while also acting as interviewer on set, where my approachable and empathetic demeanor fosters an environment of comfort, confidence, and clarity for the speaker. While many academic professionals are seasoned public speakers, the presence of cameras can induce a momentary lapse in composure. I strive to cultivate a stress-free atmosphere, allowing speakers to pause and rephrase as needed, ensuring they project their utmost confidence. Multiple takes are often captured to guarantee the speaker's optimal presentation.

Post-production starts out with a review of everything that was captured during production, followed by the enhancement of the captured footage through color grading. Next comes the editing of interview sound bites and b-roll to craft a succinct monologue or dialogue. Once we’ve achieved “picture lock”, I integrate opening and closing graphics, lower-thirds for speaker identification, and animated infographics to facilitate the audience's comprehension of complex topics. Throughout this process, stakeholders are invited to provide feedback at key stages, ensuring the final product remains aligned with its objectives.

Once we have a final video, ready for release, I collaborate with your team to select and configure an appropriate video hosting platform, and to integrate the video into your website or social media campaign. A crucial, often overlooked, step is the implementation of user metrics and subsequent analysis of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). This enables us to extract valuable insights, fostering informed decision-making for future projects and establishing a framework for continuous improvement.

Producing a video of professional caliber will increase audience engagement, but can be a complex endeavor. Without a solid plan, costs can escalate rapidly, and the final product may fall short of its intended purpose. Allow me to partner with your team, guiding you through each phase of the project, to create a video that stands out, while also achieving your objectives and fulfilling the needs of your audience.
