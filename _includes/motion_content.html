Motion Graphics & Animation

Motion design that makes complex ideas clear, in whatever voice the brand speaks.

I design and animate graphics packages, show opens, lower thirds, open and close stingers, infographics and explainer animation for broadcast, digital and social. Much of that work lives inside an existing brand: a client's style guide, a series look, a public TV show package. I study what makes a design and motion language work, then extend it so each new piece feels like it belongs.

When a project needs a new direction, I start with the idea: style frames, storyboards and motion tests that let the team see where a piece is going before full animation begins. From there I take it through to final delivery, with close attention to typography, composition, pacing, music and sound.

My video motion tools are After Effects and Blackmagic Fusion. I design characters, then rig and animate them with RubberHose and Duik. When a piece calls for 3D, I model in Blender and bring those elements into the same animation and compositing workflow. I also handle green screen keying and rotoscoping.

Interactive and web animation

Not all motion lives in a video player. I build SVG and vector animation for the web with GreenSock (GSAP) and other JavaScript libraries, and with Adobe Animate: animation that responds to scrolling, clicks and choices, from simple icons to full illustrations.

  • Web Animation Showcase: scroll-driven animation built with GSAP and ScrollMagic.
  • Remember this? A small decade-guessing game with animated type and SVG.
  • Emanuel Garden: a family garden book whose corner vines are drawn and grown in the browser.

A newer addition to my workflow is AI coding agents, for expressions, scripts, tools and interactive builds like these. Years of creative work, a grounding in code and training in UX/UI design let me direct them as an advanced tool, not a replacement for thinking: the strategy, design and creative direction stay mine.
