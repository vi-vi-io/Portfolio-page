Plan Education Site

Retirement planning, piece by piece: a plan education site for Johnson County, Kansas employees.

Your Retirement Plan, Piece by Piece is an education website for Johnson County, Kansas employees, many of whom had never considered planning for retirement. It opens with a simple question, “Do you find retirement planning puzzling?”, then breaks retirement income into four pieces of a puzzle: Social Security, KPERS (the Kansas Public Employees Retirement System), the county's 457(b) and 401(a) plans, and personal savings.

Each piece has its own color, carried from the puzzle graphic into its section of the site, so employees always know which part of the picture they're reading about. The pages work on any device, with plain-language explanations, enrollment basics, links to the plan overview video and to employees' accounts, and charts such as a breakdown of where the average household's money goes, based on Bureau of Labor Statistics data.

My role

I designed the site, including the color system that ties each section to its piece of the puzzle, and hand-coded it. The puzzle illustrations and the copy came from others on the team.

The site and the Johnson County Series of participant videos came from separate campaigns for the same employees, and each one drew on the other.
