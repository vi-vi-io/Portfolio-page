About me

Hello, my name is Scott. I'm a Visual Storyteller who makes complex topics clear for real audiences. I start with strategy: shaping the idea, planning the production and designing how the message will land. Then I bring it to life through motion graphics, animation, interactive video and full-process video production, explaining what matters, from retirement saving and investment decisions to public services, education and science.

" ... I bring this spirit of adventure, along with thought leadership, and roll-up-the-sleeves tenacity to every project I work on."

As an independent professional, I work with financial services firms, government agencies, educational organizations and companies in the pharmaceutical and chemical industries, for clients across the US, the UK and India. My nonprofit and community media work includes a Habitat for Humanity success story. I work alongside the executives, lawyers and public officials who review and approve the message, and from concept to delivery, I'm dedicated to providing solutions that meet or exceed their goals.

When I'm not behind a camera or at a computer screen, I look for opportunities to enjoy camping, sailing and other outdoor exploration. I bring this spirit of adventure, along with thought leadership, and roll-up-the-sleeves tenacity to every project I work on.
