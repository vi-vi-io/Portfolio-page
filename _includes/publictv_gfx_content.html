The Register’s Report

The Register's Report GFX package (00:47)

A new graphics package for The Register’s Report, an existing monthly series produced with Plymouth Area Community Television (PACTV). Episodes cover current news, guest segments and the history behind documents held in our country’s oldest registry.

The challenge

There was no budget for a custom package. And because the graphics change every episode, the producers needed something their own team could update each month, without the time or After Effects experience to rework complex animation.

The approach

What we did have was rare access to film inside the Registry, with Registrar John Buckley as our guide. I built the package around that footage and delivered it as Adobe Motion Graphics Templates (MOGRTs), so the team could update each episode’s graphics in Premiere Pro without opening After Effects.

The result

Careful footage selection, deliberate typography and a working knowledge of how MOGRTs are built under the hood produced a repeatable package with far higher production value than off-the-shelf templates.
