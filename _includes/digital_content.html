Digital Design

The web stands at the dynamic intersection of form and function, seamlessly blending code, graphics, video, and the written word to create immersive and interactive experiences

I see Digital Design as a canvas where technical precision meets creative expression, enabling the construction of visually compelling and highly functional platforms that inform, engage, and inspire users across the globe. To achieve this, I use a wide array of technologies, combined with an intuitive design aesthetic, and empathy with the end user.

UX/UI design

I completed my BFA at Lesley University in 2025 with two graduate-level independent studies in UX/UI design, the second building on the first. My advisors and I chose that path as the most useful addition to an established career, and it paid off right away in my professional work.

The same user-centered thinking now shapes my strategy for digital and video alike: start with who will use the site or watch the video and what they need from it, then design the structure, flow and interface around them.
