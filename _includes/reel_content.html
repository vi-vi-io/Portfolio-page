Demo Reel (01:20)

Eighty seconds of filming, editing and motion graphics, drawn from financial services, government, education and community television productions.

The range is the point. Some of this work had to live inside an existing look: a client’s brand guidelines, a series format, a show’s graphics package. Some of it started from a blank page. Either way, the job was the same: understand what the piece has to do and who it’s for, then make it clear, engaging and true to the brand.

A reel shows the craft. The case studies below show the thinking behind it: how each project was planned, shot, cut and animated, and what it achieved.
