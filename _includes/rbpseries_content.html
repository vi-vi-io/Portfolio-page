Saving for Retirement Best Practice video series

A video series on the importance of saving for retirement, told in the voices of Johnson County, Kansas employees: part of a campaign that raised plan participation 18%.

Series pilot (03:15)

Johnson County, Kansas wanted more of its employees saving in the county's retirement plan, with Voya Financial. Working as part of a broader team, I helped create a series that makes the case for saving in the voices of the county's own people: a series pilot and seven vignettes of individual plan participants from a variety of public employee roles.

The approach

Instead of a spokesperson, the series lets employees explain in their own words why they save. A colleague in a similar role makes a more convincing case than a message from an employer or plan provider. The range of people we filmed also broadened my own view of the many forms a plan participant can take.

Planning and production

I joined the planning from the start to bring a post-production perspective, so we captured what the edit would need. On the shoot, I ran camera and took part in the interviews.

The project brought together a planning committee and many contacts across the county, which had its challenges. A careful planning process kept expectations attainable while leaving the team flexible enough to seize opportunities to exceed them, and we avoided serious problems along the way.

Post-production

I worked on post-production across the series, editing and creating graphics within both Voya's and the county's brand guidelines.

The result

The videos ran in email campaigns and enrollment packages, on digital education sites and on the plan's access website. The campaign they were part of raised plan participation by 18%.
