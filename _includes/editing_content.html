Video Editing

A visual storyteller who shapes real footage into clear, compelling stories.

I edit with story, pacing, music and sound in mind from the first assembly. Unscripted work is one of my strengths: working through interviews and b-roll to find the arc and the moments that land, then building graphics and sound into the cut instead of laying them on top.

My editing spans an in-house corporate agency and an independent practice: documentary, journalistic, interview, educational, corporate and promotional work, from multi-camera interview series to short-form pieces for the web and community television.

Much of it lives inside an existing look and voice: a client's brand guidelines, a series format, a show's style. I take projects from rough cut through review rounds to final delivery, turning feedback from producers, executives and legal reviewers into revisions that keep the story intact.

Because I also shoot and light, I edit with the shoot in mind, and I plan coverage on set with the edit in mind.

I cut in DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere Pro, with After Effects for graphics. For quick-turnaround edits I work with the Blackmagic Speed Editor, which keeps assembly and trimming fast on deadline. I'm comfortable in whichever NLE your team has standardized on.

Short-form for social

Vertical video for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts has its own rules: the message has to land in the first seconds, often with the sound off, in a frame taller than it is wide. I plan for that from the concept, letting on-screen type, motion and pacing carry the story.

National Voter Registration Day, WeVote (00:20)

Part of a short, nonpartisan campaign for WeVote in the weeks before National Voter Registration Day 2024, alongside email, print and social outreach. The campaign became a template for later get-out-the-vote efforts. As volunteer video producer, I led the strategy, concept and script with WeVote's marketing team and founder, applying techniques I'd used to raise retirement plan participation, then edited the piece from stock footage and designed its graphics. It ran on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
